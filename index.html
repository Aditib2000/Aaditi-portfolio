About Me

I'm a Data Science graduate student at the University of North Texas (GPA 3.9/4.0) and hold a Master's in Computer Science from H.V. Desai College, India, with hands-on experience across analytics, business intelligence, and applied machine learning. I like problems that involve turning a pile of messy, real-world data into something a business can actually act on — a dashboard, a model, a recommendation.

My background spans building predictive models and interactive dashboards to guide product and marketing strategy, as well as two years as a Power BI Analyst at HiLine Solutions, where I built dashboards and DAX-driven reporting for cross-functional teams. Outside of work, I lead marketing and communications for UNT's Graduate Student Council.

Lately I've been deep in LLM-powered tooling — building an end-to-end NLP pipeline that cleaned 800K+ records with GPT, and an AI resume/ATS matcher that uses local embedding models instead of paid APIs.
